Rules:

  1. Every voice counts equally. Major decisions are made by discussion first, then a vote. Each of the four members has one vote, and a decision needs at least three in favour.
  2. Raise issues early, and openly. Problems are discussed directly with the people involved within 48 hours, and kept about the work, not the person. No silent resentment, no side talks.
  3. Unresolved conflicts go to a team review. If two members cannot settle a disagreement, the other two mediate. The outcome is recorded, everyone commits to it, and we revisit it after one month.
